# Music Notes

Little music notes (♪ ♫ ♬) drift up around her while she dances to your music, and stop when the music does.

**Options:** most notes at once · how long a note floats · how fast they rise

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Quaisar_THM
- Needs: Capri with mod API 3 (0.8.7 or newer), Windows

### Why the mod check flags it
- **Calls Windows directly**: to keep the notes out of screenshots and screen captures, including her own glances at your screen.

No network access, no keys, nothing sent anywhere.
