# Reaction Memory

Capri's reactions, greetings and check-ins don't go through chat, so her brain never sees them. Reply "why'd you say that?" and she has no idea what "that" was.

**Reaction Memory** keeps the last lines she said out loud on her own and adds them to what her brain is told with each message, marked as her own words. Answer a reaction and she knows what you're on about.

**Options:** how many lines (12) · how long she remembers them (30 min) · also include her chat replies

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Quaisar_THM
- Needs: Capri with mod API 3 (0.8.7 or newer), Windows
- Permissions: voice,chat

### Why the mod check flags it
- **Changes what her brain is told**: that's the point; it adds the lines she said out loud, marked as her own words.

The mod makes no network requests. Saved lines become part of the prompt for your selected brain, including when you use a cloud provider.
